🚀 **Work in Progress: Hermes - AI-Powered Education Assistant**

I am developing a web-based dashboard that professionalizes education coaching through data and artificial intelligence. Hermes leverages custom markdown profiles to memorize students' individual skills (such as reading speed, focus duration, and test-taking abilities) to generate fully personalized, dynamic weekly study plans.

Upcoming Features:

    🎯 Dynamic Planning: AI-driven study schedules that automatically adjust based on the student's target-vs-actual performance from the previous week.

    📊 Detailed Analytics: Weekly progress reports calculating success rates broken down by subject and topic.

    🔔 Automated Notifications: An assistant interface that detects student absences and instantly drafts notification messages for parents.
